# Third-party components

- Three.js 0.187.0: MIT; https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/LICENSE . Used for rendering and OrbitControls. Exact package version and integrity are locked.
- Vite 8.3.5: MIT; https://github.com/vitejs/vite/blob/main/LICENSE . Development/build tooling and its dependencies retain their own licenses in npm packages.
- DM Sans and Manrope are loaded through Google Fonts. Their font licenses and author information are available in the Google Fonts repository: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope .
- All terrain, trees, buildings, water, clouds, brand graphics and simulation logic in this repository are code-authored for this implementation. No third-party photographic assets or prerecorded simulation video are bundled.

Related watershed projects informed the research/positioning notes; their code is not copied into this implementation.
